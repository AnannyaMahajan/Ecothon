Proposed Solution

A **Community + AI based monitoring system** that detects water hyacinth spread in water bodies and transforms scattered citizen reports into an actionable, real-time dashboard.

How it Works

  • Citizens upload photos of lakes, rivers, and ponds.
  • AI analyzes images to calculate vegetation coverage and classify water body health as Healthy, At Risk, or Critical.
  • Each report is geo-tagged and plotted on a live map dashboard.
  • Authorities monitor infestation hotspots in real time and plan targeted interventions.

Why it Matters

  • Enables early detection of water hyacinth spread.
  • Provides precise location data for faster, more efficient cleanup.
  • Helps authorities prioritize critical areas using AI-based severity scoring.
  • Bridges citizens and authorities for sustainable water management.
Early Detection Real-time Geo Mapping AI-assisted Decisions Community Participation

Innovation & Uniqueness

  • **Community + AI Integration** for environmental monitoring, moving beyond slow manual surveys.
  • Uses simple, explainable computer vision instead of complex, costly ML black boxes.
  • Real-time, geo-tagged reports create a live infestation map accessible to authorities.
  • Unified platform that connects public participation with a government-facing dashboard.
  • Designed as a low-cost, scalable solution that can extend to other invasive species.

Technical Approach

A modern, lightweight stack built with open-source tools, optimized for real-time reporting and analysis.

Languages & Frontend

  • Python – Backend logic & AI image processing
  • JavaScript – Frontend interactivity & map updates
  • HTML5 & CSS3 – Responsive, accessible UI

Frameworks & Tools

  • FastAPI – High-performance REST backend
  • OpenCV – Image analysis & vegetation detection
  • NumPy – Efficient image data processing
  • Leaflet.js – Interactive geo-mapping dashboard

Data & Deployment

  • SQLite – Lightweight report & location storage
  • Browser Geolocation API – Automatic GPS detection
  • REST APIs – Seamless frontend–backend integration
  • Runs on standard laptops/PCs and mobile devices

Feasibility, Risks & Mitigation

Feasibility

  • Technically practical on normal computers.
  • Low cost, built with free and open-source technologies.
  • Easy to use with just a smartphone for reporting.
  • Scales from city pilots to national deployments.
  • Operationally useful for real-time authority action.

Challenges & Risks

  • Inaccurate or blurry citizen-submitted images.
  • GPS inaccuracies from mobile devices.
  • Dependence on stable internet connectivity.
  • AI misclassification in complex environments.
  • Low citizen participation affecting coverage.
  • Data privacy and security concerns.
  • Ongoing maintenance and model updates.

Mitigation Strategies

  • Image quality prompts and guidelines in the app.
  • Auto GPS validation with map preview and confirmation.
  • Offline capture with delayed upload for low-connectivity areas.
  • Continuous AI model improvement with new field data.
  • Engagement features: rewards, badges, awareness drives.
  • Strong data security, encryption, and privacy compliance.
  • Expert authority verification for critical AI detections.

Impact & Benefits

Turning every citizen into an environmental guardian while giving authorities the data they need to act quickly and effectively.

Environmental

  • Early detection slows invasive species spread.
  • Protects aquatic life and biodiversity.
  • Prevents water blockage and ecosystem damage.

Social

  • Citizens actively contribute to conservation.
  • Raises awareness about water health and invasives.
  • Strengthens collaboration between communities and government.

Economic

  • Reduces large-scale cleanup and survey costs.
  • Minimizes losses in fisheries, agriculture, and tourism.
  • Saves manpower required for manual field surveys.

Governance

  • Delivers real-time data for better planning.
  • Enables faster, data-driven decision-making.
  • Increases efficiency of environmental monitoring.
